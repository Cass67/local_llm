## Deployment verification

The PR image is active on cluster `0663b08f` with `DS4_INDEX_TC=1`.
Image index: `dbc9d5c7a37cb3c42f5975ef24cc3f8d7152776fb54383beeb22f5a0c689a0b7`.

All four RX 7900 XT cards passed all 12 standalone cases (48 total), including
1.0000 top-512 agreement. The worst relative score difference was 3.48e-7.
Arithmetic, a forced tool call and strict JSON-schema output passed.

Two fresh synthetic inventory prompts were compared with the previous
v1.1.50 image, temperature 0, thinking budget 0, 128 output tokens, and no
prompt-cache reuse. Both versions retrieved all three planted audit codes.
Compilation finished before the baseline was measured.

| Prompt tokens | Baseline prefill tok/s | PR prefill tok/s | Change | Baseline decode tok/s | PR decode tok/s |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 31866 | 483.7 | 462.0 | -4.5% | 33.9 | 34.3 |
| 130170 | 470.3 | 463.2 | -1.5% | 33.6 | 32.2 |

These are single comparisons across two model loads, not repeated matched
A/B trials. Startup calibration, expert-cache state and generated text may
affect results. They do not demonstrate an end-to-end speedup on this host,
despite faster standalone indexer timings. The PR remains enabled as the
requested experimental build; the previous image and profile remain available.

No other open upstream PR was added. The model's six experts, context,
DSpark, sampling and existing optimization settings are unchanged.
